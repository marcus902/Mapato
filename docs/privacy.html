Mapato - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 August 2026

Malik Tech ("we", "us") operates the Mapato mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices regarding that data.

1. Data the App Accesses

  • SMS messages (if you enable SMS capture): The App reads incoming SMS messages from your device to detect and record mobile money and bank transactions (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, Tigo Pesa, CRDB, NMB, and others). SMS content is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded or shared with any server.
  • Notifications (if you enable notification access): The App reads notifications from mobile money and banking apps to automatically detect transactions. Notification content is processed locally and is never uploaded or shared.
  • Transaction data: The App stores transaction records (amount, network, category, counterparty, date/time) in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

2. AI Chat Feature

If you use the built-in AI chat, transaction summaries (amounts, categories, and totals — not raw SMS or notification text) are sent to Groq (groq.com) to generate financial insights. No personally identifiable information, phone numbers, or message content is sent. You may disable the AI feature at any time in Settings.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identifiers (name, phone number, email).
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect usage analytics or crash reports.
  • We do not share any data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • We have no servers — all data stays on your device.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. The App does not transmit data to any external servers except for the optional AI chat feature described above. You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

5. Permissions

  • READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS: Used solely to detect mobile money and bank transactions from incoming SMS messages.
  • Notification Access: Used solely to detect transactions from mobile money and banking app notifications.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to show you a notification when a transaction is detected.
  • INTERNET: Used only for the optional AI chat feature.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
